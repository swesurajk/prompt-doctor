AI provider

Prompt Copilot calls your provider directly with your own key. Nothing passes through any server we run.
Must expose POST {baseUrl}/chat/completions. Works with Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM and most gateways. You will be asked to grant access to this host.
A small fast model is usually enough. Analysis is one request per click.
Stored with chrome.storage.local, never synced, and readable only by the extension's service worker — never by a page you visit.

Behaviour

Sent with every analysis. Keep it short — it costs tokens on every request.

Privacy

Your prompt leaves the browser only when you click ✨ Improve, and only to the provider you configured above. Prompt Copilot has no backend, no analytics and no telemetry. Before sending, it scans locally for API keys, tokens and card numbers and warns you.
